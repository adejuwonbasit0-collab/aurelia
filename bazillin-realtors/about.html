How we handle the risky part

Most property disputes start with a document nobody checked. Before any payment we encourage you to confirm the seller is who they say they are, the survey matches the land, and the title is clean at the land registry. We recommend a lawyer for the search and we do not ask you to rush.

Questions people ask us

Can I see a property before paying anything?

Yes. We arrange the viewing first. Payment is only discussed after you have seen the property and the documents.

What documents should I ask for when buying?

Ask for the title document (such as a Certificate of Occupancy or Governor’s consent), the registered survey plan and the deed of assignment. Have a lawyer run a search at the land registry before you pay.

What do I pay up front when renting?

Usually the yearly rent plus agency and legal fees. The exact amounts differ by landlord and city, so we give you an itemised list before you commit.

Can I pay in installments?

Some sellers and developers offer payment plans, especially on land and new builds. Ask us about the listing you like and we will confirm the terms in writing.

Do you help diaspora buyers?

Yes. We can do video viewings on WhatsApp, share documents for your lawyer to review and update you at each step.

How do I list my property?

Send the location, photos, size, documents and asking price on WhatsApp. We will confirm the details with you and publish the listing.
